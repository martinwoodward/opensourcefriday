In this special edition of Open Source Fridays, we celebrate Maintainer Month and Global Accessibility Awareness Day (GAAD). Andrea is joined by Joe Devon, tech entrepreneur and co-founder of GAAD, and Ed Summers, blind developer and head of accessibility at GitHub. They discuss the origins of GAAD, the importance of accessibility in technology, and GitHub's pledge to advance accessibility in open source projects. Joe and Ed share personal stories and valuable resources for developers to improve accessibility in their projects. The episode also highlights the GAAD pledge and how individuals can contribute to making digital content accessible for all.

Accessibility Tools and Resources
- Magenta Ally: https://magenta.ally/
An open-source project by T-Mobile providing practical accessibility testing guidance, sample code, and videos demonstrating assistive technology usage.
- Atomic Ally: https://atomic.ally/
A forked version of Magenta Ally by Charlie Triplett, offering developer-friendly resources for building accessible components.
- Deque University: https://dequeuniversity.com/
Affordable online courses for developers to learn accessibility fundamentals, recommended by Joe Devon for its practical approach.

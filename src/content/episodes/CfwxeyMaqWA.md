Join us for an Open Source Friday livestream with Peter Thomas, the creator of the open-source Karate framework, in conversation with Karan M V from GitHub. We’ll explore Peter’s journey into the world of open source—what sparked his initial interest, how Karate was born out of real-world testing challenges, and why he chose to make it open to the community. From humble beginnings to becoming one of the most widely adopted test automation tools, Karate’s story is one of passion, innovation, and the power of community-driven development.

We’ll also dive into the evolution of Karate Labs, the company behind the project, and what it means to turn a successful open-source project into a sustainable business. Expect insights on the balancing act between open source and commercial growth, the lessons learned along the way, and advice for anyone looking to follow a similar path. Whether you're an open source contributor, a test automation enthusiast, or just curious about how great projects come to life, this is a conversation you won’t want to miss.

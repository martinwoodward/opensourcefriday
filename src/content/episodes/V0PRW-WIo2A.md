Join us on this episode of Open Source Friday as we welcome Laurent Cozic, the creator of Joplin—a powerful open-source tool for note-taking and organization with an emphasis on user privacy. Laurent shares his journey in developing Joplin, discusses its unique features such as end-to-end encryption and plugins, and demonstrates its capabilities on both desktop and mobile applications. Learn about Joplin's integration with various synchronization services, the community-driven plugin ecosystem, and their exciting future plans for the application. Perfect for anyone looking to control their data and enhance their productivity with open-source solutions.
